# English narration

Target duration: 75 to 85 seconds at a calm, clear pace.

## Script

Requests rarely arrive as clean engineering tickets. They start in chat, email, meetings, or support conversations.

In Intake, an engineering lead captures the original message and connects it to the right project. AI proposes a clear title, summary, request type, and any questions that still need an answer. Nothing leaves the system yet.

The lead reviews the proposal, adjusts the priority, chooses GitHub as the delivery target, and makes the final decision. Intake then creates a real Issue from the approved snapshot, with a traceable link back to the original request.

In Daily Planning, the lead assigns the Issue to Maya, chooses the morning slot, and plans three hours against the team's available capacity.

As the work moves forward, Intake keeps the request, decision, Issue, plan, and delivery evidence in one visible journey. When the work is reported complete, the lead can accept it or return it for another pass.

One request. One traceable workflow. Human judgment at every critical step.

Intake is self-hosted, open source, and integrates with GitHub or GitLab.

## Delivery notes

- Voice: neutral international English.
- Pace: approximately 125 to 135 words per minute.
- Tone: calm, practical, and confident.
- Do not read button labels unless the corresponding action is visible.
- Leave a short pause before the final product statement.
